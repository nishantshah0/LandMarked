# The Claim Walk — morning of judging

Greedy nearest-first route from Stackt through all 40 landmarks (~7.1 km total).
**Don't do all of it.** Stops 1–20 are ~2.5 km ≈ 45 minutes and give the demo
everything it needs. Use "Walk me there" in the app — that's what it's for.

## Split by zone (2–4 people, different handles)

- **Zone A — Fort York loop (stops 1–12):** Fort York, the gun, the 1812
  monuments, Little Norway, June Callwood Park, Queen's Wharf Lighthouse
- **Zone B — Bentway strip (stops 13–20):** LAV III, Rangers Museum, the
  Gardiner murals, the Bentway, Garrison Common, Ordnance Park
- **Zone C — King West (stops 21–35):** Factory Theatre, St. Andrew's
  Playground, Emergence, then the **Draper Street goldmine — five pins in one
  block (stops 28–32)**, Victoria Memorial Square
- **Zone D — CityPlace (stops 36–39):** Canoe Landing, Rosa Nautica, Light
  Canoes, Flower Power

## Rules that make the data demo well

1. **Different handles per person.** Standings with one name look like one name.
2. **Space the bursts.** A round now, a round mid-morning, a round after noon —
   each becomes its own row in the hour-by-hour colour view.
3. **Manufacture one rivalry honestly:** two people claim the same pin hours
   apart (a hold must lapse or stay within the 3 h window — re-claims of a
   *freed* pin count). "Most contested" needs at least one place with history.
4. **THE VENUE PIN STAYS FREE AFTER NOON.** Holds last 3 hours. If someone
   claims "SummerHacks @ Stackt Market" at 12:30, it's locked until 3:30 and
   no judge can claim it live. Claim it early morning for the data, then leave
   it alone — it's the judges' pin.
5. Photograph the actual place, not your face — the archive is public and it
   is the product.
